# Maintainers

| Maintainer | GitHub | Since | Can |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colorado Startups, owner | @Colorado-Startups/maintainers | 2026 | Everything: merge any PR, promote to production, change these rules |

AI agents review and merge AI-mergeable PRs ([`tools/merge_policy.json`](tools/merge_policy.json), beside `tools/merge_policy.py`). They are not maintainers. Their review is the approval for every PR they merge, including a PR the orchestrator rebuilt from a fork. They change the rules they follow only in a pull request that Colorado Startups asked for.

To become one, see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md#the-maintainer-seat).
