PASS if the reply gives the plan as a table (or an equally scannable list) of small, PR-sized steps with the files each touches and what each depends on, and then lists a short brief for each independent step that the person can paste into a session themselves, and the reply shows one `Checked:` line with a pass or fail mark for each of True, Whole, Small, Equipped and Owner.
